## The PCBWay sponsorship

I was on my way preparing the new H3S-Dev board - with MCU ESP32-S3, LoRa SX1262 and other features. PCBWay marketing representative contacted me again offering their professional services with sponsorship for a turnkey prototype production lot of the board - both boards, components and assembly!

### [PCBWay](https://www.pcbway.com/) offered a turnkey service to manufacture and assemble my new design. The whole operation was fast and reliable since every single step was agreed between the customer (me) and the responsible people from the factory. Several component issues were resolved with deep understanding of my needs as a customer at a high professional level.

Ordering of the boards and assembly service was easy to use and fully automated through the PCBWay website and was conducted smoothly. The forwarding options were fast and flexible to have my prototypes on time!

I was quite impressed by the way of work and communication with the PCBWay team. The communication was fast and focused on the customer's needs.

## Boosting the new version

That opportunity boosted up the project’s new version to be released and soon the design was ready for production. Together with the PCBWay team all the details regarding component selection, exact model part numbers were negotiated smoothly. Boards were ready on time and the delivery was very fast.
